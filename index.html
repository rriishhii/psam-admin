About this app

Property Survey & Assessment Manager (PSAM) is a data entry tool for Urban Local Bodies.

  • Keeps a record for every property, identified by Ward and GIS ID.
  • Records floor-by-floor details.
  • Produces ward-wise reports.
  • Exports and imports the full database.
